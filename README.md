Kestrel Run is a small prop-plane courier game set in Alder Valley: take off from Alder Field, thread every glowing ring over the river and the ridges, mind the fuel and the stall, and bring the Kestrel down gently at North Field. Three sorties — a dawn run, a low river run, and a gusty dusk ridge run — each with its own rings, light and weather.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

## How to play

- **Pitch:** ↑ / ↓ (or W / S) · **Bank:** ← / → (or A / D) · **Throttle:** Shift up, Ctrl down · **Mouse:** hold the pointer off-centre to steer.
- **Gear:** G (won't come down too fast) · **Pause:** P · **Mute:** M · **Restart:** R · **Sortie:** 1 / 2 / 3.
- Take off past 34 m/s with the nose up; below 26 m/s the wing stalls and the nose drops.
- Fly through the gold hoop for points and fuel; the closer to the middle, the more it pays.
- Land on the runway, gear down, gentle and slow, after the last ring. A belly landing, a hard touchdown or the hillside ends the sortie — a soft meadow landing counts, with a penalty.
- Fuel drains with throttle; rings top it up. The clock gives you five minutes a sortie.
